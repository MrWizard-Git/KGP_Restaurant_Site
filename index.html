About Rajnawabi

A Grand Unit of Rajbari Heritage

The royal Rajnawabi restaurant is renowned for traditional Mughal cuisine. We are delighted to welcome our guests and exceed all your expectations with an opulent dining ambiance crafted with elaborate wooden carvings, shimmering sconces, and golden brassware.

We are waiting for you every day to indulge in the royal flavors of dum biryanis, aromatic kebabs, and rich Mughlai gravies prepared with authentic royal heritage recipes.
